# Themes

GameVault 1 ships with three themes and can load community and premium themes.
Pick one in **Settings → Application → Theme**.

:::note
This page covers GameVault 1. GameVault 2 currently ships light and dark themes
only.
:::

## Free themes

- **Default (Dark)**: the default theme from the 2024 GameVault rebrand.
  ![Default (Dark)](/img/themes/default_dark.png)
- **Default (Light)**: the light variant of the same rebrand.
  ![Default (Light)](/img/themes/default_light.png)
- **Classic (Dark)**: keeps the colours of the pre-2024 design.
  ![Classic (Dark)](/img/themes/classic_dark.png)

## Community themes

Community themes are installed from the theme repository: open the community
theme list in **Settings → Application**, pick a theme and press **Install
theme**. Imported themes appear in the installed list, where you can also
uninstall them or open the theme folder.

## Premium and custom themes

Premium themes and custom themes require a
[GameVault+](../../gamevault-plus/introduction.md) subscription. The catalogue
and the format for custom themes are documented under
[GameVault+ themes](../../gamevault-plus/themes.md).

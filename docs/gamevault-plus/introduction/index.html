GameVault+

GameVault+ is the optional subscription for GameVault. It funds development and adds extra features; the server and clients stay free without it. Features are added as they are finished.

GameVault 2

GameVault+ is currently available in GameVault 1. Support for GameVault 2 is coming soon. The feature comparison lists every GameVault+ feature and its tracking issue.

Plans start at €4.99 per month. Subscribe to GameVault+

Features​

  • Animated profile pictures
  • Cloud saves
  • Playnite integration
  • Steam integration
  • Discord integration
  • Additional built-in and custom themes
  • Client APIs for integrations
  • Multiple profiles per installation

Pricing​

Three plans:

  1. Flex plan: €4.99 per month for one person.
  2. Smart saver plan: €49.90 per year for one person.
  3. Family & friends plan: €8.99 per month for you and up to 5 others.

Get started​

  1. Sign up or sign in to your Phalcode® account.
  2. Subscribe to a GameVault+ plan.
  3. Enable GameVault+ in your client.

Questions and answers​

What is Phalcode®?​

Phalcode® is the company behind GameVault: two friends in Germany writing the software. More on our website.

I have trouble with my subscription and I need support. How can I contact you?​

Write to us on Discord or by email. Keep subscription and billing questions out of the public Discord channels.

How do I manage billing and payments?​

Billing runs through Stripe. Manage the subscription, invoices and payment details in the Customer Portal.

What payment methods are accepted for GameVault+ subscriptions?​

Credit and debit cards, digital wallets, bank redirects and SEPA direct debit.

Delayed confirmation

Multibanco, Bancontact, iDEAL, SOFORT and SEPA direct debit can take longer to confirm. Pick a method that confirms immediately if you want the subscription right away.

Are there going to be more features added to GameVault+?​

Yes. Features are added as they are finished.

Can I share GameVault+ with others?​

Sharing depends on your subscription plan:

  • Family & friends plan: share with up to 5 people through the Manage members button on the Phalcode® account page.
  • Flex and smart saver plans: one person, no sharing. Sharing the Phalcode® account itself is not allowed either.

How can I manage members of my Family & Friends subscription?​

Open the Phalcode® account page. Shareable subscriptions show a Manage members button, which opens a dialog for up to five people. Changes take about five minutes to apply.

Does GameVault+ support multiple GameVault Servers?​

Yes. GameVault+ works on every server you connect to, but admins can turn individual features off on their server, save file uploads for example.

How many devices can I use with my GameVault+ subscription?​

There is no device limit. Sharing your Phalcode® account with other people is not allowed.

Can GameVault+ be used offline?​

Setting up the licence needs an internet connection once. After that GameVault+ works offline for the rest of the subscription period. An annual plan therefore only goes online once a year.

How does GameVault+ handle my data?​

Data is encrypted and handled under the GDPR. Billing and payment data sits at our payment provider Stripe, which is PCI compliant; see Stripe's security page. The privacy policy has the details.

Will GameVault+ support more integrations with other software in the future?​

Probably. We are talking to other platforms and tools about integrations.

Can users suggest or vote on new features for GameVault+?​

Yes, though not every idea makes it in. Post suggestions on GitHub or Discord.

Which client versions are supported?​

GameVault+ needs the official client, version 1.9.0.0 or newer, from the Microsoft Store or from another installation option.

Are bulk or commercial licences available?​

Yes, through an individual agreement: write to us on Discord or by email. Personal and family plans are meant for private use.

What if I can't afford GameVault+ or don't want to purchase it?​

That's okay! We understand that not everyone can afford GameVault+. Developing and maintaining the service comes with costs, and while we aim to keep the core features free and accessible to all, some convenience features are behind a paywall to help cover these expenses.

If you're unable to purchase it, you're welcome to explore ways to work around the paywall for personal use. The source code is available on GitHub. However, please avoid pirated versions, as they may contain malware, and please do not share any bypass modifications.

Why is there no lifetime license?​

A one-time payment does not cover servers, updates and new features, which cost money every month. GameVault+ is a thank-you for continuous support, and a lifetime licence would remove that link.

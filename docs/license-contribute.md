# Licensing and contributions

## License

GameVault is licensed under the [Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/), with the exception of some components such as extensions and plugins.

You may use, adapt and share the work if you credit it, keep it non-commercial
and license derivatives the same way.

This also means that **GameVault is not considered open-source**.

### Why is GameVault not open-source?

People often read the public code as open source. It is source-available: you can
read and modify the code, but the licence does not grant the open-source
freedoms. Keeping it that way lets us show the code and still stop others from
selling it. See the [2023 licence announcement](/blog/2023/07/13).

## Contributing to GameVault

Anyone can contribute.

Ways to help that need no code:

- Rate GameVault in the Microsoft Store, or recommend it to someone.
- Write about your setup on a blog, a forum, Reddit or social media.
- Mention GameVault in a talk, a podcast or a video.

For code, open a pull request in the repository you want to change. For bugs and
ideas, open an issue with enough detail to reproduce the problem.

Before submitting code, documentation, or other copyrightable material for inclusion,
review the Contributor License Agreement in the relevant repository:

- [Application](https://github.com/Phalcode/gamevault-app/blob/develop/CONTRIBUTING.md#contributor-license-agreement-cla)
- [Frontend](https://github.com/Phalcode/gamevault-frontend/blob/develop/CONTRIBUTING.md#contributor-license-agreement-cla)
- [Backend](https://github.com/Phalcode/gamevault-backend/blob/develop/CONTRIBUTING.md#contributor-license-agreement-cla)
- [Documentation](https://github.com/Phalcode/gamevault-docs/blob/develop/CONTRIBUTING.md#contributor-license-agreement-cla)

You retain ownership of your contribution and grant Phalcode the rights described
in that agreement, including sublicensing, relicensing, and commercial distribution.
Knowingly submitting a contribution after receiving clear notice of the agreement
and an opportunity to review it indicates acceptance of its terms.

## Publishing Adaptations of GameVault

The licence forbids commercial adaptations. Non-commercial derivatives are
allowed under the conditions below.

GameVault is developed commercially. Third-party software may not use the
trademarks GameVault™ or Phalcode® without our consent, and we enforce that to
keep unauthorised products from being associated with us.

Derivatives must not look like official products. Users otherwise assume we
endorse or maintain them, which becomes a support and safety problem: as a German
GbR we carry personal liability for our products, so we keep control of the brand
and the software.

Derivatives are welcome under a distinct name such as "GameVaulTux" or
"GameVault Snake Edition". The product description has to say that they are
unofficial.

Publishing an official product under our brand requires a formal agreement that
names Phalcode® as sole rights holder and transfers liabilities, rights and
responsibilities to us.
